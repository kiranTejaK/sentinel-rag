# SentinelRAG Workspace Directives & Documentation Rules

## Daily Documentation & Interview Questionnaire Policy
1. **NO DSA / LeetCode in Daily Logs:**
   - Never write Data Structures & Algorithms (DSA), LeetCode problems, or algorithmic toy puzzles in daily logs.
2. **Comprehensive Topic-Wise Interview Questionnaire:**
   - Daily logs must contain a thorough, multi-topic interview questionnaire with exhaustive, production-grade answers (not just 2-3 questions).
   - Must organize questions by topic (Python/ASGI Concurrency Internals, Container & Linux Security, Networking & Protocols, Database & pgvector Internals, Distributed Caching & Redis, Resilience & Reliability).
   - Must go beyond project-specific questions to address foundational computer science, OS/network-level mechanics, high-concurrency failure modes, and architectural trade-offs that staff/principal engineering interviewers demand.
